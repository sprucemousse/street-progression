9-Street Pause System

Fixed 9-street coverage (1,2,4,5,7,9,10,11,12 — never rolls). Double on the 1st loss; from the 2nd loss, each win closes roughly half of whatever is currently owed — it does NOT clear in a clean 2 wins, it gradually closes the gap over several rounds, which is what keeps the stake small. After 9 losses in a row, stop betting and just watch until 3 real wins happen in a row, then resume exactly where the recovery left off.
The safest version tested — zero busts across all 32 real datasets (including two separate sessions each from 5 different casinos). It is also slow: typical sessions gain roughly 5–20%, not fast wins. No progression removes the house edge.
